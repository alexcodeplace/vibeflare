## Bounded streaming for the actual complete screenshot inventory

Run39120799783 again passed all122browser cases and allunit/audit checks but
its full screenshot inventory exceeded the newly invented16MiB aggregate
transport cap. The original optional GitHub artifact action did not have that
aggregate cap. Rather than holding all images in memory or dropping any, the
transport now inventories at most256PNGs/128MiBraw, transmits at most64MiB of
unique PNG bytes, and processes only one at a time under the unchanged4MiB
per-file bound. SHA256 deduplication and gzip preserve every original image,
while stdout backpressure prevents an unbounded queue. The corrected inventory
limits are explicit; they are not represented as unchanged16MiBlimits. Actual
runner CPU/RAM budgets, required browser assertions and timeouts are unchanged.

A source-bound pending manifest cannot assert retention. The final completion
frame is emitted only after every unique payload; altered files and interrupted
transport refuse completion. Regression tests reconstruct exactoriginalPNGbytes,
prove repeated images share one payload, and retain input/unique-byte limits.
The original failures remain in GitHub; full current-source CI is required.
